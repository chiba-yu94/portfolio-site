MataneNote
Privacy policy

MataneNote (the “app”) handles information only to the extent necessary to provide the user experience.

1. Information handled

The app handles the note information users enter in the app.

2. Purpose of use

This information is used to save, display, and edit notes on the user’s device.

3. External transmission

The app does not send user-entered content to the developer’s server. Processing by the OS or store provider, such as Apple’s payment features, may occur.

4. Third-party provision

The app does not provide user information to third parties.

5. Contact

For questions about this policy, please contact:
