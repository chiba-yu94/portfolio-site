A SMALL TOOL FOR A REAL TASK

Pick up the next conversation with a little more context.

For customer-facing work where remembering the last conversation matters. Keep short notes about past conversations, future topics, and topics to avoid, then search before the next visit. Available on the Japan App Store.

What it does

  • Organize previous conversations, future topics, and topics to avoid for each customer
  • Search notes before a conversation
  • Keep notes on the device
  • Optional Pro removes the display limit and enables QR note handoff

GOOD TO KNOW

Common questions

What does the free plan include?

The free plan displays up to 15 entries. Pro removes this display limit and enables QR note handoff.

How much is Pro?

On the Japan App Store, Pro is an auto-renewing subscription of ¥500 monthly or ¥4,800 annually. Both plans have the same features. Manage or cancel in device settings.

Where are notes stored?

Notes are stored on your device and are not sent to the developer’s server. QR handoff is a Pro feature.
