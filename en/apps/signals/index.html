A SMALL TOOL FOR A REAL TASK

Find a small creature in an everyday code.

A collection game about discovering and observing little code creatures. The same barcode brings back the same creature, and one favorite can accompany you on Apple Watch. Available on the Japan App Store.

What it does

  • Discover creatures by scanning or entering a barcode
  • Meet the same creature again from the same code
  • Observe movements and tap reactions in the lab
  • Bring one favorite to Apple Watch and supported watch-face complications

KNOW THE SCOPE

Important limitations

  • Apple Watch is optional; Watch features need watchOS11+ and a paired iPhone
  • Watch-face complications are static; animation runs inside the Watch app
  • Does not search product names, manufacturers, or product images
  • No individual deletion or export; deleting the app may remove records
PRIVACY, EXPLAINED

Privacy characteristics

Observation records stay on the device. Camera images and raw codes are not stored or sent. No accounts, ads, analytics SDKs, or developer cloud. Selected creature details transfer to the paired Watch.

GOOD TO KNOW

Common questions

Do I need an Apple Watch?

No. Apple Watch is optional. You can discover and observe creatures on iPhone by scanning or entering codes.

Is camera permission required?

No camera permission is needed when you enter codes manually.

Are watch-face creatures animated?

Watch-face complications are static. Animation runs in the Watch app. Watch features require watchOS 11 or later and a paired iPhone.
