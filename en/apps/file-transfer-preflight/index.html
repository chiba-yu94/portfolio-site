A SMALL TOOL FOR A REAL TASK

Catch filename and path issues before a handoff.

For people delivering folders or ZIPs to clients and colleagues. Review destination-specific naming rules, path lengths, and collisions locally before you share a file.

What it does

  • Windows / Office, OneDrive / SharePoint, and cross-platform ZIP profiles
  • Check reserved names, invalid characters, long paths, and case or Unicode collisions
  • Estimate resulting paths using an optional destination root
  • Review explanations and suggestions; export CSV or HTML reports

Important limitations

  • Checks names, paths, and metadata rather than file contents or recipient-app compatibility
  • Does not modify originals; ZIPs are inspected without extraction
  • Up to 100,000 entries; does not guarantee transfer success
PRIVACY, EXPLAINED

Privacy characteristics

Local inspection with no network access, account, advertising, or analytics. Exported names and paths may themselves be confidential.

GOOD TO KNOW

Common questions

Does it change my files?

No. It never renames, deletes, moves, or rewrites originals. ZIP files are inspected without extraction.

Does it connect to OneDrive or SharePoint?

No. It applies the selected destination rules to names and paths locally on your Mac.

Does a clean result guarantee a successful transfer?

No. It does not validate file contents or opening files in recipient apps. Findings are guidance for review before handoff.
