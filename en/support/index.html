CSV Preflight

Open or drop a CSV to inspect it. The first row is treated as a header. For unsupported encoding, export a new UTF-8 or BOM-marked UTF-16 file from the original tool.

Features & limitationsProduct support & usage notes ↗Privacy policy ↗
Does it repair CSV files?

No. CSV Preflight inspects files and exports reports. It does not automatically repair or rewrite the original CSV.

Which encodings are supported?

UTF-8 and BOM-marked UTF-16. Other encodings are reported as unsupported.

Do files leave my Mac?

Inspection is local. No account or cloud upload is needed.

macOS

File Transfer Preflight

Choose a folder or ZIP and a destination profile, then scan. The destination root is optional. For cloud profiles, use the decoded library path rather than a local sync folder or URL.

Features & limitationsProduct support & usage notes ↗Privacy policy ↗
Does it change my files?

No. It never renames, deletes, moves, or rewrites originals. ZIP files are inspected without extraction.

Does it connect to OneDrive or SharePoint?

No. It applies the selected destination rules to names and paths locally on your Mac.

Does a clean result guarantee a successful transfer?

No. It does not validate file contents or opening files in recipient apps. Findings are guidance for review before handoff.

iOS

MataneNote

Add topics for each customer and use search to find them. Manage Pro subscriptions in device settings. Please do not include real customer names or notes in support messages.

Features & limitationsProduct support & usage notes ↗Privacy policy ↗
What does the free plan include?

The free plan displays up to 15 entries. Pro removes this display limit and enables QR note handoff.

How much is Pro?

On the Japan App Store, Pro is an auto-renewing subscription of ¥500 monthly or ¥4,800 annually. Both plans have the same features. Manage or cancel in device settings.

Where are notes stored?

Notes are stored on your device and are not sent to the developer’s server. QR handoff is a Pro feature.

iOS

SIGNALS: Code Creatures

You can enter a code manually if scanning is unavailable. One favorite can be sent to Apple Watch. Check that the Watch is paired and runs watchOS 11 or later.

Features & limitationsProduct support & usage notes ↗Privacy policy ↗
Do I need an Apple Watch?

No. Apple Watch is optional. You can discover and observe creatures on iPhone by scanning or entering codes.

Is camera permission required?

No camera permission is needed when you enter codes manually.

Are watch-face creatures animated?

Watch-face complications are static. Animation runs in the Watch app. Watch features require watchOS 11 or later and a paired iPhone.
